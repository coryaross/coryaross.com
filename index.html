About

A marketer who can operate where the story is still forming.

I work best at the edge of new markets: AI, Web3, fintech, venture, privacy, and other categories where the product is technical, the audience is skeptical, and the narrative has to be precise.

I have led marketing at Shima Capital, Discreet Labs / Findora, Mogul Productions, and Peerplays, often as the first senior marketing hire or the person brought in when the story, team, or market needed sharper operating discipline. My work spans brand strategy, PR, GTM, portfolio advising, community growth, reputation management, campaign design, executive voice, and hands-on AI-assisted building.
